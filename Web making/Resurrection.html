Resurrection of Jesus

- After Jesus was crucified, his body was placed in a tomb, and a large stone sealed the entrance.

- Early on the third day—Sunday—Mary Magdalene and other women went to the tomb.,

- According to the Gospels, they found the stone rolled away and were told that Jesus had risen from the dead.
- Jesus later appeared to Mary Magdalene and his disciples.

- He reassured them that he was alive and called them to share his message.

- For Christians, the resurrection means that death does not have the final word.

- It offers hope, forgiveness, and the promise of eternal life. “He is not here; he has risen!” — Luke 24:6
