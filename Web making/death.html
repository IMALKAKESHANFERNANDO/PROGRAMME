Death of Jesus

He was betrayed by Judas and arrested. — Matthew 26:47–50

- Pontius Pilate ordered His crucifixion. — Mark 15:15

- Christians believe He died for our sins. — 1 Corinthians 15:3

- He was buried and rose again on the third day. — 1 Corinthians 15:4

- Soldiers mocked Him and put a crown of thorns on His head. — Matthew 27:27–31

- He was crucified between two criminals. — Luke 23:32–33

- Darkness covered the land before He died. — Mark 15:33–37

- He said, “It is finished,” and gave up His spirit. — John 19:30

- The temple curtain tore in two. — Matthew 27:51

- Joseph of Arimathea placed His body in a tomb. — Matthew 27:57–60

- Christians believe His death shows God’s love and brings forgiveness of sins. — Romans 5:8; Ephesians 1:7
