Ministry of Jesus

He was baptized by John the Baptist. — Matthew 3:13–17
- He chose twelve apostles to share His message. — Luke 6:12–16
- He taught through parables, such as the Good Samaritan. — Luke 10:25–37
- He fed the hungry, including a crowd of five thousand. — Matthew 14:13–21
- He welcomed children and blessed them. — Mark 10:13–16
- He served others, washing His disciples’ feet. — John 13:1–17
- He sent His followers to make disciples of all nations. — Matthew 28:19–20
